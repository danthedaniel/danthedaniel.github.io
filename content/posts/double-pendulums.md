A year ago I watched [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtjb2OhEQcU) about how double pendulums aren't always chaotic.

<!--more-->

Depending on the initial conditions they can be surprisingly non-chaotic. Even more interesting, there are little islands of stability in otherwise chaotic regions of the parameter space. I wanted more control to explore the 2D map shown in the video, so I built my own tool. It's hosted at [doublethe.fun](https://doublethe.fun) and is [open source](https://github.com/danthedaniel/doublethe.fun). The core was written last year. I finally got around to adding an explanation and a major optimization thanks to Fable 5.
